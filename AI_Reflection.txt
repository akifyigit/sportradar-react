In this project, I used AI as a support tool to help me when I encountered challenges or needed clarification.
The way I use AI is very similar to how I used to rely on Google, documentation websites, and Stack Overflow.
Instead of manually searching through multiple pages, AI helped by summarizing information quickly and pointing me in the right direction.
It did not replace my work, but it helped me work more efficiently.
One of the main benefits I experienced was faster debugging.
During the process of building components and writing tests, I encountered issues that would normally take long web searches to resolve.
By asking AI about a test failure or an unexpected React behavior, I was able to understand the root cause more quickly.
Even when the suggestions were not directly applicable, they helped me think about the problem in a different way.
AI also helped me stay up to date with current practices. Web development changes rapidly, and tools like React, React Router, and Testing Library often introduce new patterns.
I used AI to confirm whether certain patterns were still recommended or if newer approaches existed.
This allowed me to avoid outdated methods and ensured that the project stayed aligned with modern standards.
However, AI did not write the entire solution for me.
I structured the project, wrote the components, implemented the logic, designed the pages, and handled the state management myself.
When AI generated a suggestion, I evaluated it like any other external source; sometimes I used it, sometimes I modified it, and sometimes I discarded it. 
This was important to ensure that I fully understood the code and that it met the project requirements.
Overall, my use of AI was similar to using a very fast, interactive documentation assistant. It supported learning, improved my workflow, and reduced repetitive searching. 
But the responsibility for writing, understanding, and validating the final project remained entirely mine. 
I see AI as an extension of the developer toolbox. Useful, efficient, but not a replacement for my own reasoning or coding skills.
